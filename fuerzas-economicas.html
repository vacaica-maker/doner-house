Shift to a Service Economy: GDP

Colombia's economy has shifted decisively toward services, which have consistently represented more than 58% of national GDP, according to DANE (Colombia's national statistics department). Within that segment, food and accommodation services — including quick-service restaurants (QSR) — are a key growth driver: the total foodservice market exceeds USD 21 billion, with the QSR segment growing at an estimated 5.8% CAGR. This growth is fueled by last-mile delivery platforms, rising urban density, and process optimization — all factors directly relevant to a QSR concept like Döner House.
