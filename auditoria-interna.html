3. Perform Internal Audit

David's Strategic Management frames the internal audit around the basic business functions — management, marketing, finance/accounting, production/operations, R&D, and management information systems — to surface the firm's own strengths and weaknesses. This page covers the Management function, starting with how Döner House is organized.

← Back to Index

3.3 Management

David identifies five basic management functions — planning, organizing, motivating, staffing, and controlling — that together make up the first internal-audit area a firm should examine, because how well a company performs these functions determines whether every other function (marketing, finance, operations) can actually be executed. Döner House is a single-store, founder-led operation, so the function with the most immediate strategic weight at this stage is organizing: deciding who does what, who reports to whom, and how the seven roles that run the store day to day fit together.

3.3.2 Organizing

David defines organizing as encompassing organizational structure, job design and specialization, span of control, chain of command, and coordination among positions and departments — in short, the design work that turns strategy into a set of roles people can actually be hired for. For Döner House, the Founding Team retains strategic and financial oversight while delegating full day-to-day operational authority to a single Store Manager, who in turn supervises four functional leads and their supporting staff. This flat, single-layer chain of command keeps the span of control wide (one manager over four leads) but manageable for a one-store operation, and avoids the coordination overhead of a taller hierarchy the business does not yet need.

Organizational Structure

  • Founding Team Strategic & financial oversight
    • Store Manager Reports to Founding Team
      • Kitchen & Quality Lead Reports to Store Manager
        • Assembly Assistant Reports to Kitchen & Quality Lead
        • Dishwasher & Cleaning Assistant Reports to Kitchen & Quality Lead
      • Cashier & Customer Service Lead Reports to Store Manager
        • Packaging & Dispatch Reports to Cashier & Customer Service Lead
      • Purchasing & Finance Lead Reports to Store Manager
      • Sales & Social Media Lead Reports to Store Manager

Job Descriptions

The seven positions below define Döner House's job design in full — title, terms, salary band, purpose, responsibilities, and candidate requirements for every role in the structure above.

1. Store Manager

Location: Bogotá, Colombia Terms: Permanent / Full-time (40 hrs/week) | Mon–Sat, 11 AM–6 PM Salary: COP $2,300,000 – $2,700,000
About the Role

To oversee all daily operations of the doner kebab shop, ensuring profitability, quality standards, customer satisfaction, and compliance with health regulations. Reports to Founding Team.

Responsibilities
  • Manage monthly budget, cash flow, and operational profitability
  • Supervise and support 4 team leaders and operational staff
  • Ensure consistent product quality and customer service standards
  • Implement and maintain operational processes and systems
  • Monitor costs and work with finance team on budget management
  • Report on key performance indicators to Founding Team
  • Ensure food safety and INVIMA compliance at all times
Candidate requirements
  • 2–3 years of operations management experience in food service or hospitality
  • Experience in a fast-paced restaurant or similar environment
  • Understanding of budgeting and cost control principles
  • Leadership and team motivation skills
  • Familiarity with POS systems
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously

2. Kitchen and Quality Lead

Location: Bogotá, Colombia Terms: Permanent / Full-time (40 hrs/week) | Mon–Sat, 11 AM–6 PM Salary: COP $1,800,000 – $2,100,000
About the Role

To prepare authentic doner kebap and lead kitchen operations while maintaining consistent quality, food safety, and training of kitchen team members. Reports to Store Manager.

Responsibilities
  • Prepare doner kebap daily using proper meat selection, preparation, and cooking techniques
  • Maintain consistent flavor, portion sizes, and presentation quality
  • Supervise hygiene standards and food safety compliance
  • Train and guide kitchen staff on food preparation and safety procedures
  • Monitor and communicate daily ingredient needs to purchasing team
  • Maintain a clean, organized, and safe kitchen environment
  • Control product quality and minimize waste
Candidate requirements
  • 2+ years of cooking experience in a professional kitchen
  • Experience with meat preparation and/or grilled foods preferred
  • Food handler certification or willingness to obtain it
  • Ability to teach and guide other team members
  • Knowledge of basic food safety and hygiene practices
  • Attention to detail and commitment to consistency

3. Cashier and Customer Service Lead

Location: Bogotá, Colombia Terms: Permanent / Full-time (40 hrs/week) | Mon–Sat, 11 AM–6 PM Salary: COP $1,600,000 – $1,900,000 (plus commission)
About the Role

To provide excellent customer service at the counter, manage cash transactions, and coordinate food delivery and packaging. Represents the brand and ensures customer satisfaction. Reports to Store Manager.

Responsibilities
  • Serve customers with professionalism and efficiency
  • Take doner kebap orders and customize according to customer preferences
  • Operate cash register and handle various payment methods
  • Reconcile cash at end of shift
  • Encourage customers to add extra items to their orders
  • Address customer questions and concerns
  • Coordinate food packaging and delivery for orders
  • Support the packaging and dispatch team
Candidate requirements
  • 1+ years of customer service experience in food retail or restaurant
  • Experience using a cash register or POS system
  • Friendly, professional attitude and good communication skills
  • Ability to handle money accurately
  • Willingness to suggest additional products to customers
  • Ability to work in a busy environment

4. Purchasing and Finance Lead

Location: Bogotá, Colombia Terms: Permanent / Full-time (40 hrs/week) | Mon–Sat, 11 AM–6 PM Salary: COP $1,700,000 – $2,000,000
About the Role

To manage ingredient sourcing and purchasing, maintain financial records, prepare monthly reports, and optimize costs to ensure profitable operations. Reports to Store Manager.

Responsibilities
  • Purchase ingredients and supplies from approved vendors
  • Work with suppliers on pricing and delivery schedules
  • Track ingredient costs and monitor spending
  • Prepare monthly financial reports including profit and loss summaries
  • Create budget forecasts and cash flow projections
  • Monitor inventory and identify cost-saving opportunities
  • Maintain financial records and documentation
Candidate requirements
  • 2+ years in purchasing, accounting, or business administration
  • Experience in food service or restaurant business is helpful
  • Comfortable with Excel and basic accounting software
  • Understanding of budget and cost management
  • Ability to communicate clearly with suppliers
  • Strong attention to detail and accuracy with numbers

5. Sales and Social Media Lead

Location: Bogotá, Colombia Terms: Permanent / Full-time (40 hrs/week) | Mon–Sat, 11 AM–6 PM Salary: COP $1,500,000 – $1,800,000 (plus commission)
About the Role

To promote doner kebap through social media, build customer relationships online, and develop business growth through marketing and corporate event sales. Reports to Store Manager.

Responsibilities
  • Create and share content on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and Google Business
  • Respond to customer comments and messages on social media
  • Plan and execute social media campaigns and promotions
  • Manage paid advertising on Facebook, Instagram, and Google
  • Work with food influencers and delivery app partners
  • Develop catering and corporate event business
  • Track performance of social media campaigns and marketing efforts
  • Suggest seasonal promotions and special offerings
Candidate requirements
  • 1+ years of experience in marketing, social media, or sales
  • Experience managing social media accounts for a food or restaurant business preferred
  • Ability to use social media platforms (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
  • Basic understanding of paid advertising platforms
  • Strong written communication skills
  • Comfortable with analytics and tracking results
  • Spanish and English language skills

6. Assembly Assistant

Location: Bogotá, Colombia Terms: Permanent / Full-time (40 hrs/week) | Mon–Sat, 11 AM–6 PM Salary: COP $1,200,000 – $1,450,000
About the Role

To assemble and plate doner kebap sandwiches quickly and accurately, prepare fresh vegetables and sauces, and support kitchen operations. Reports to Kitchen Lead.

Responsibilities
  • Assemble doner kebap sandwiches with proper portions and presentation
  • Prepare fresh vegetables, sauces, and accompaniments
  • Keep work station organized and clean during service
  • Receive and store fresh ingredients properly
  • Follow food safety and hygiene guidelines
  • Support kitchen team with cleaning and preparation tasks
Candidate requirements
  • 6+ months of food preparation experience in a kitchen
  • Food handler certification or willingness to obtain it
  • Ability to work quickly while maintaining quality
  • Attention to detail in food preparation
  • Comfort working in a fast-paced kitchen
  • Willingness to follow instructions and learn new tasks

7. Dishwasher and Cleaning Assistant

Location: Bogotá, Colombia Terms: Permanent / Full-time (40 hrs/week) | Mon–Sat, 11 AM–6 PM Salary: COP $1,100,000 – $1,300,000
About the Role

To maintain cleanliness and hygiene standards throughout the restaurant by washing dishes, sanitizing equipment, and cleaning bathrooms and dining areas. Reports to Kitchen Lead.

Responsibilities
  • Wash dishes, pots, pans, and utensils
  • Sanitize kitchen equipment and work surfaces
  • Clean and maintain public bathrooms
  • Empty trash and keep common areas clean
  • Follow food safety and hygiene protocols
  • Support kitchen team as needed
Candidate requirements
  • Previous experience in cleaning or food service is helpful but not required
  • Responsibility for maintaining cleanliness standards
  • Ability to follow instructions and procedures
  • Willingness to work with cleaning chemicals and equipment
  • Positive attitude and teamwork
