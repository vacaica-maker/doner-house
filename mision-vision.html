1. Develop Vision and Mission Statements

The purpose and strategic direction of Döner House: its history, mission and vision.

1.1 History

The Döner Kebab was born in Berlin in the 1970s, when Turkish migrants turned a traditional grilled meat dish into the wrapped, sauce rich street food that became Germany's most popular fast meal. Half a century later it is part of everyday life in almost every German city, yet in Colombia it remains almost unknown.

That contrast is where our idea started. As five students working together on a business project, we set out to design a company from the ground up, and the Döner gave us a product with a rare combination of qualities, familiar in one part of the world, genuinely new in another. One of us, an exchange student from Germany, brought the firsthand experience of what a good Döner should taste like and how central it has become to German food culture. The rest of the team brought the local perspective on Bogotá, its fast food scene, and what young Colombians actually want to eat.

Out of that mix came a simple goal, to introduce Colombia to an authentic Döner Kebab as a quick, consistent, and affordable everyday choice. We are starting small and deliberately, with a single store in a busy part of Bogotá. From there, one satisfied customer at a time, we intend to make the Döner as familiar here as it already is in Germany.

1.2 Mission Statement

Mission

To satisfy our (1) customers by offering an authentic culinary experience centered around our signature (2) product: delicious, German-inspired Döner Kebabs made with high-quality ingredients. Operating from our physical storefront, we are guided by a strong philosophy of integrity and culinary excellence (6), supported by our distinct self-concept of European flavor agility (7). Driven by a deep concern for employees who form the empowering backbone of our firm (9), setting us apart in the local fast-food sector.

1.2.2 Principal Characteristics of the Mission

  • 4. Technology — We excluded this as our process is purely artisanal. Every Döner Kebab is prepared by hand using traditional methods, and highlighting technology would detract from our authentic, handmade craft.
  • 5. Growth and Profitability — While financial sustainability is vital for any new business, we omitted these terms to ensure our mission remains a warm invitation to our customers rather than a corporate financial report.
  • 8. Public Image — As a growing local shop in Bogotá, we are currently focused on building direct connections with our community. We chose to prioritize the guest experience over large-scale social campaigns during this foundational stage.

1.3 Vision Statement

Vision

To become Bogotá's authentic döner kebab restaurant (3), recognized for offering authentic German-inspired flavors (6), creating a welcoming place for students and families (1) to enjoy quality food (2) while building a trusted and respected brand (7).
