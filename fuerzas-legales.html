2.3 Political, Legal and Governmental Forces

Analysis of the political, regulatory and governmental framework that governs Döner House's commercial activity.

← Back to Index

Resolución 2674 de 2013

Issued by Colombia's Ministry of Health and Social Protection, this resolution sets the sanitary requirements for manufacturing, processing, preparing, packaging, storing, transporting and selling food and raw materials in Colombia, including hygiene and sanitary-condition controls and INVIMA sanitary registration. It applies directly to Döner House's kitchen operations and food handling practices.

Resolución 5109 de 2005

Issued by Colombia's Ministry of Social Protection, this technical regulation sets the labeling requirements for packaged food products and raw materials, requiring clear and accurate ingredient information and prohibiting false or misleading claims. It applies to any packaged item Döner House sells (e.g., bottled drinks, packaged sauces).
